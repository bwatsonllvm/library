One surface, two source types, shared research threads

MLIR work tends to move fluidly between presentations, slide decks, design meetings, and publications. This area keeps that motion intact so you can move from a talk to a paper, then into the same people and key topics, without changing context.

Start anywhere. Talks capture live engineering decisions and demos. Papers preserve the deeper written record. Together they form one browseable MLIR research surface inside the library.
